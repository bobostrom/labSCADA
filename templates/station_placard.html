Manual Dipstick Standard Operating Procedure

Mon / Wed / Fri by 5:00 PM
  1. PPE: Put on insulated cryogenic gloves and protective face shield.
  2. Measure Level: Lower measuring ruler straight into tank until resting flat on the bottom plate. Hold 5 seconds.
  3. Read Frost: Withdraw ruler, wave in air 3 seconds, and immediately read the frost line in centimeters (cm).
  4. Scan QR: Open your smartphone camera and point at the code on the left.
  5. Select Tank & Submit: Tap Cryo Tank 1 or Cryo Tank 2 at the top of your phone screen. Enter the measured cm and your initials, then tap Submit.
  6. Log Adjacent Tank: On the confirmation screen, tap "Log Another Tank" to immediately record the second tank without re-scanning.
🛡️ CAP / CLIA Compliance: Digital clinical audit trail with immutable timestamps.
⚠️ Critical Minimum: 10.0 cm (Refill required if level drops below 12.0 cm)
